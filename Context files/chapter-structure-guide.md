# 🧱 Structure Guide for Software Development Thesis / Project Report

This guide outlines what each chapter in a typical software development thesis or documentation should contain. Use it as a checklist when writing.

---

## 1. Einleitung (Introduction)

**Purpose:** Introduce the reader to the topic and motivate the project.

### Include:
- **Context:** Where the topic fits (academic, industry, user need)
- **Problem Statement:** What problem you aim to solve
- **Objectives / Research Questions:** What you plan to achieve
- **Scope:** What’s included/excluded in this work
- **Structure of the Document:** Brief overview of chapters

---

## 2. Grundlagen (Foundations / Background)

**Purpose:** Establish theoretical and technological groundwork.

### Include:
- **Domain knowledge:** Definitions, stakeholders, system context
- **Related Work:** Existing solutions, academic papers, tools
- **Technologies:** Frameworks, libraries, protocols used
- **Methods:** Models, design patterns, or theories applied

---

## 3. Methodologie (Methodology)

**Purpose:** Describe your development process.

### Include:
- **Approach:** Agile, waterfall, or hybrid?
- **Requirements Gathering:** Surveys, interviews, personas, etc.
- **Design & Architecture:** System models, component overview
- **Implementation Notes:** Tech stack, tools, coding standards

---

## 4. Anforderungserhebung und -analyse (Requirements Analysis)

**Purpose:** Detail the user and system requirements.

### Include:
- **Stakeholders:** Who needs what?
- **Functional Requirements:** What should the system do?
    - Must-have (MUSS)
    - Nice-to-have (KANN)
- **Non-functional Requirements:** Performance, security, usability, etc.
    - Must-have (MUSS)
    - Nice-to-have (KANN)

---

## 5. Umsetzung (Implementation)

**Purpose:** Present how the system was built.

### Include:
- **System Overview:** Key modules, services, workflows
- **Screenshots / UI Designs:** With descriptions
- **Challenges & Solutions:** What you encountered and how you solved it

---

## 6. Evaluation / Test / Ergebnisse

**Purpose:** Show how the system was tested and evaluated.

### Include:
- **Test Plan:** Manual, automated, unit, integration
- **Test Results:** Coverage, bugs found, user feedback
- **Metrics:** Performance benchmarks, UX scores, etc.
- **Limitations:** What doesn't work or could be improved

---

## 7. Datenschutz und ethische Aspekte (if applicable)

**Purpose:** Ensure responsibility in handling data and users.

### Include:
- **Data Protection:** GDPR compliance, anonymization
- **Ethics:** Consent, bias, fair use of AI/data

---

## 8. Fazit und Ausblick (Conclusion & Outlook)

**Purpose:** Reflect and summarize your work.

### Include:
- **Summary of Results:** What was achieved
- **Answer to Research Questions**
- **Lessons Learned**
- **Future Work:** What remains or can be extended

---

## 📎 Appendices (optional)

### May include:
- Source Code Excerpts
- Additional Diagrams
- User Surveys / Interview Guides
- Glossary
- Bibliography (via `.bib` or inline)

---

## 🔄 Notes

- Always cite your sources.
- Use diagrams and tables where needed.
- Align structure with university or supervisor requirements.
