\section{Robustness}
\label{sec:robustness}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Honest parallel trends.} \textcite{rambachan2023} breakdown
    value $\bar{M}$ for each headline result.
  \item \textbf{Control validity.} Level shift in control-only imports at
    $t^{*}$; re-estimation on the non-substitutable subsample
    (\Cref{sec:substitution}).
  \item \textbf{Placebo dates.} January 2024 and January 2025.
  \item \textbf{Winsorisation.} Unit-value trimming at the 1st/99th and
    5th/95th percentiles.
  \item \textbf{Estimator.} \acrshort{ols} on logs of positive flows, to show
    what the \acrshort{ppml} choice is worth.
  \item \textbf{Leave-one-sector-out.} Re-estimation dropping each of the six
    covered sectors in turn, since steel is large enough to drive the pooled
    result on its own.
\end{enumerate}
